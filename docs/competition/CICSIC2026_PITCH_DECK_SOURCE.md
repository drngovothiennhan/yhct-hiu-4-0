## Competition title

**YHCT Connect x Smart Herb**  
**A Free Phygital Learning Ecosystem for Traditional Medicine Students**

Tagline: **Traditional Medicine Knowledge for Every Student**

Core principle: **Near - Simple - Free**

## Slide 1 - Title

YHCT Connect x Smart Herb  
A Free Phygital Learning Ecosystem for Traditional Medicine Students

One-line value proposition: **Scan a real medicinal herb, access structured Traditional Medicine knowledge, trace modern evidence, learn safely, and continue the discussion in one student-centered academic platform.**

## Slide 2 - The problem

Traditional Medicine learning is fragmented.

A student may need one place for the physical specimen, another for lecture notes, a search engine for documents, PubMed for research evidence, a generic social network for discussion, and a generic AI tool for explanations.

The result is not necessarily lack of information; it is a broken learning workflow.

## Slide 3 - Why this matters for students

Students need a workflow that is:

- close to how they already learn;
- simple enough for first-year use;
- accessible on an ordinary phone;
- academically traceable;
- explicit about medical-safety limits;
- free at the core learning layer.

## Slide 4 - Our solution

YHCT Connect is the academic-social and research platform. Smart Herb is its tangible physical-to-digital learning layer.

**Physical specimen -> QR -> structured herb profile -> Traditional Medicine context -> modern evidence -> safety note -> micro-quiz -> academic community.**

## Slide 5 - Smart Herb live product

Show the three implemented MVP specimens:

- Đương quy
- Sinh khương
- Cam thảo

A student scans a QR code with the native phone camera. No dedicated app is required. The herb profile is publicly readable without login.

## Slide 6 - Student journey

**Discover -> Scan -> Read -> Verify -> Quiz -> Discuss -> Research**

The product reduces context switching and makes evidence retrieval part of the normal learning path.

## Slide 7 - Academic social network

YHCT Connect provides the community layer around learning:

- public academic reading;
- member discussion and interaction;
- research discovery;
- saved learning resources;
- academic profiles and community participation.

The competition version keeps Guest access open for public learning while login is reserved for participation and contribution.

## Slide 8 - AI Academic Copilot

AI is positioned as an academic assistant, not a doctor.

Appropriate roles:

- explain concepts;
- summarize research;
- connect Traditional Medicine concepts with traceable sources;
- support retrieval and study;
- surface safety reminders.

Not permitted:

- autonomous diagnosis;
- individualized prescriptions;
- treatment guarantees;
- replacement of teachers or clinicians.

## Slide 9 - Technology architecture

User layer: mobile-first responsive web experience.

Product layer: YHCT Connect academic platform + Smart Herb deep links.

Data layer: Supabase database, authentication, RLS, storage/realtime where required.

Evidence layer: PubMed-linked sources and curated Traditional Medicine learning content.

Deployment/quality layer: GitHub CI, rendered Chrome QA and isolated Vercel preview before production merge.

## Slide 10 - Innovation

The innovation is not simply the use of AI.

The product combines:

**Physical medicinal-herb learning x academic social UX x evidence traceability x AI-assisted study x safety boundaries.**

This creates a concrete learning journey rather than another standalone chatbot or document library.

## Slide 11 - Near - Simple - Free

**Near:** starts from the physical herb and familiar smartphone behavior.

**Simple:** scan one QR; no special device or app installation is required for the MVP.

**Free:** core student learning access is designed to remain free, supported by low-cost/open-source/free-tier infrastructure where practical.

## Slide 12 - Social and educational impact

The intended impact is improved accessibility to structured Traditional Medicine learning and evidence-oriented study.

Permitted current claim: a functional staging prototype has been built and technically validated.

Do not claim measured learning improvement, adoption scale, diagnostic accuracy or clinical outcomes until real pilot data exist.

## Slide 13 - Sustainability

**Free for Students - Sustainable for Institutions.**

Potential future institutional model:

- university partnerships;
- institutional deployments;
- sponsored research/education programs;
- advanced institutional services.

The core student knowledge-access layer remains the mission-critical free component.

## Slide 14 - Roadmap

Stage 1: three-herb competition MVP with QR, evidence, safety and quiz.

Stage 2: 10-20 common herbs and structured student pilot.

Stage 3: larger curated herb catalog, AI Academic Copilot integration and institutional pilot.

Stage 4: cross-university Traditional Medicine learning network and multilingual expansion.

NFC may be explored later; QR remains the current low-cost standard.

## Slide 15 - Team and vision

Vision: **Traditional Medicine Knowledge Should Be Accessible to Every Student.**

Closing line:

**YHCT Connect x Smart Herb turns a real medicinal herb into an immediate doorway to structured knowledge, modern evidence, safe learning and an academic community.**

## 60-second English pitch

Traditional Medicine contains centuries of knowledge, but for today's students, learning it is surprisingly fragmented. A physical herb may be in the laboratory, notes are somewhere else, research evidence is in PubMed, discussion happens on social media, and explanations come from generic AI tools.

We created YHCT Connect x Smart Herb, a free phygital learning ecosystem for Traditional Medicine students.

Students simply scan a QR code on a real medicinal-herb specimen. On an ordinary phone, without installing a separate app, they can open a structured herb profile, learn the Traditional Medicine context, trace modern evidence, see safety notes, complete a micro-quiz, and continue into an academic community.

Our principle is Near, Simple and Free. AI assists learning and evidence retrieval; it does not diagnose or replace clinicians.

Our vision is simple: Traditional Medicine knowledge should be accessible to every student.

## Jury-safe claim boundary

Current evidence supports describing the product as a functional staging prototype with verified software QA and traceable source links for the three competition demo herbs.

Do not present unmeasured user counts, learning gains, clinical outcomes, AI accuracy percentages, university adoption figures, revenue, or contracts.
