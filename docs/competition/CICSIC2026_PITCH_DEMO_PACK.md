# CICSIC 2026 — Pitch & Demo Pack

## One-line product definition

**YHCT Connect + Smart Herb is a free phygital learning ecosystem that connects real medicinal-herb specimens to structured Traditional Medicine knowledge, modern evidence, safety context and student academic interaction through a simple QR scan.**

## Core positioning

Do not present the product as "a social network with AI". Present it as a real student learning workflow:

**Physical herb → QR → structured knowledge → PubMed evidence → safety → micro-quiz → academic community.**

Product principle: **Near · Simple · Free.**

## 60-second English pitch

Traditional Medicine contains centuries of knowledge, but for students, learning is still fragmented. A physical herb specimen is learned in one place, notes in another, research evidence in separate databases, and discussion happens on generic social networks.

We built **YHCT Connect with Smart Herb**, a free academic social and research ecosystem for Traditional Medicine students.

A student simply scans a QR code on a real medicinal-herb specimen. Without installing another app or creating an account, the student can immediately see identification information, the part used, Traditional Medicine learning context, modern research evidence, safety notes and traceable PubMed references. A short micro-quiz reinforces learning, and YHCT Connect extends the experience into academic discussion and research discovery.

Our philosophy is **Near, Simple and Free**. AI and digital tools should make reliable knowledge easier to reach, not more expensive or more complicated.

Smart Herb is not a diagnostic or prescribing tool. It is an educational bridge between physical learning, evidence and student community.

**YHCT Connect — Traditional Medicine Knowledge for Every Student.**

## 45–60 second live demo script

**0–8s — Real object**
- Hold or show one labeled herb specimen, preferably Cam thảo or Đương quy.
- Say: "This is not only a physical specimen. It is the entry point to a structured learning experience."

**8–15s — Scan**
- Scan the QR code with a normal phone camera.
- Open `/smart-herb?herb=cam-thao` as a guest.
- Explicitly show that no login is required.

**15–30s — Structured learning**
- Show identification and part used.
- Scroll to Traditional Medicine learning context.
- Show the modern-evidence section and the evidence-level label.

**30–40s — Safety + traceability**
- Show the safety-first panel.
- Open the PubMed source briefly to demonstrate traceability.

**40–50s — Micro-learning**
- Answer the micro-quiz correctly.
- State that the purpose is learning, not prescribing or selling herbs.

**50–60s — Ecosystem close**
- Return to YHCT Connect.
- Close with: "One scan connects a real herb to evidence, safety and academic community. Near. Simple. Free."

## Key Q&A responses

### Why is this better than Google?
Google can retrieve information, but Smart Herb starts from a real learning object and gives students a structured, safety-aware, source-traceable academic pathway in one interaction.

### Why is this better than ChatGPT?
The value is not a general chatbot. The product combines physical specimens, structured herb profiles, evidence links, safety boundaries and an academic community workflow. AI is an assistant layer, not the product identity.

### Why is this better than PubMed?
PubMed is essential for evidence retrieval but is not designed as a student-facing Traditional Medicine learning experience. Smart Herb links the physical specimen and educational context directly to traceable evidence.

### Is this a medical device?
No. The current product is educational. It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend individualized treatment or claim validated clinical performance.

### Where is the innovation?
The innovation is the integration of **phygital learning + Traditional Medicine knowledge + evidence traceability + safety-first UX + academic social interaction** in a low-cost student workflow.

### How can it scale internationally?
The data model can support multilingual herb names, region-specific pharmacopeial references and university-specific teaching layers while preserving a common evidence and safety structure.
